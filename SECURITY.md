# Security Policy

This policy applies to every public Kagura AI repository that has no security policy of its own.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Report it privately in either of these ways:

- On the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**.
- Email **security@kagura-ai.com**.

We answer in English or Japanese.

## Kagura AI Cloud Edition

How we protect the hosted service is described on the [Trust & Security](https://www.kagura-ai.com/trust/) page. Our contact details are also in [security.txt](https://www.kagura-ai.com/.well-known/security.txt).
